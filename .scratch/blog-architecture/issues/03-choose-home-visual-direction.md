# Choose the Home Visual Direction

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

## Question

Which concrete home-page composition best adapts the reference site's visually varied card grid to technical content while keeping the author's engineering identity and current work immediately legible?

## Answer

The home page combines an immersive cover with an editorial content lobby on the same route. The cover is the first viewport, built around one strong image, the publication identity, and a concise positioning statement. Clicking the cover or scrolling moves directly into the content lobby; it is not a separate splash route and never becomes a mandatory extra navigation step.

The content lobby adapts the asymmetric visual rhythm of prototype A, Editorial Atlas, but organizes it around three Channels rather than a flat mixture of unrelated cards:

1. Technology Notes.
2. Life Notes.
3. Investment Notes.

Each Channel is represented by a large Home Card containing three to five recent or featured Article previews. Technology Notes contains the five previously defined technical Topics. Life Notes and Investment Notes may develop their own Topics as content accumulates.

Cards must remain easy to scan. Article previews use user-controlled horizontal scrolling or scroll snapping. A desktop-only, slow focus rotation may be considered, but it must pause on hover or focus and be disabled when the visitor prefers reduced motion. Several cards must not continuously auto-scroll at the same time.

Interaction targets are explicit: a Channel Card heading or its open action goes to the Channel index, while an Article preview goes directly to that Article. The entire composite card is not one ambiguous click target.

The persistent top navigation provides the efficient path for returning readers: Writing, Channels, Topics, Projects, About, and Search. Visitors can therefore bypass the cover and visual content lobby when they know what they need.

The evaluated throwaway prototype is [homepage-variants-v2.html](../../../.superpowers/brainstorm/visual-20260910/content/homepage-variants-v2.html). The selected direction uses Editorial Atlas as its visual foundation; the user's revised two-path concept supplies the final information hierarchy and motion boundaries.
